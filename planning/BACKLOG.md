Prefer removing repeated work over new dashboard features. Source material stays separate from AI interpretations. Prepare drafts and reports for review; never autonomously send correspondence. Human approval remains mandatory for external use, consequential decisions, and close-out. Do not merge or deploy maintenance candidates automatically.

## Definition of done

A backlog item is closed only when its approved scope is implemented, acceptance evidence is attached, negative/retry paths are exercised where applicable, confidentiality and human-approval boundaries are checked, limitations are explicit, and the owner has accepted the change. A passing test run or AI review alone is not product acceptance. Record the reviewed and delivered commit IDs, not just a date.

## Maintenance protocol

On each review, refresh main and open pull requests; update existing IDs rather than duplicate work; attach proposed decisions to DECISIONS.md; keep requirements, blueprint, acceptance and handoff together. Report meaningful changes and blockers rather than repeat the same reminders. Track admin time saved, duplicate capture rate, incomplete evidence records, stale reviews, and reminder repetition only after a baseline exists; do not invent improvement metrics.
