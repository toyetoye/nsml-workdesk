## Communication style

The system should eventually draft in a human, professional, direct, technically grounded style.

Preferred tendencies:

- polite but not weak;
- clear and practical;
- avoids unsupported certainty;
- avoids liability admissions;
- separates fact from assumption;
- gives next steps clearly.
